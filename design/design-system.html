The words are part of the design

Short declarative sentences. No em dashes in UI strings. Every alert says what happened, what we already did, and what happens next.

Like this

"Your father fell in the kitchen at 3:12. He responded to the voice check but hasn't gotten up, so we're calling you."

Never this

"EMERGENCY!!! FALL DETECTED. IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED."

Like this

"All is well. Maria was in the living room a few minutes ago."

Never this

"No anomalies detected in monitored subject activity."

Setup recommendations

  • 1

    Place the guardian at hip height

    Falls read best from 80 to 120 cm, with the whole room in frame.

  • 2

    Keep a small night light on

    The camera watches in low light, and a soft glow helps at 3am.

  • 3

    Test the alarm once a month

    One tap in settings rings your phone through silent mode.

22

See her, and be seen

During an incident, or on demand if the family allows it, you open a live window to the guardian phone. It is end-to-end encrypted and never recorded. Our servers relay the video but cannot decrypt it.

Live check-in

LIVE Encrypted
MA Maria · Kitchen

She can hear you. Speak gently.

End-to-end encrypted

Only your phone and the guardian hold the keys. The video is relayed by our servers but never decrypted, never stored, never trained on.

Every time a live window opens, who opened it and when lands in the append-only audit trail. Opening it is a logged event, the same as any escalation step.

The live feed drops into the same dark stage as guided setup. The red control ends the call; the two glass controls mute your mic and toggle the speaker.
